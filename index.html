Good to know

Frequently asked questions

When does my free trial begin?

Your 30-day trial starts when you create your first restaurant in the owner dashboard, not when you simply visit this page.

Will I be charged automatically?

No. A payment card is not required to begin and online subscription billing is not yet enabled.

Are my menu items saved after I log out?

Restaurant data is stored in Supabase cloud services and loaded when you sign in again. Data retention depends on the service and backups; no cloud service guarantees permanent storage without backups.

Can I accept online payments?

Online payment collection is not available at present. Restaurant owners can manually confirm cash received for an order.
